About

We test AI tools the way you'd actually use them.

Primeloop started from a simple frustration: most AI tool coverage is either a sponsored list or a five-minute demo dressed up as a review. Neither tells you what happens on week three, when the novelty wears off and the tool either earns its place in your day or quietly gets uninstalled.

Every review on this site comes from actually running the tool against real work for at least two weeks before we write a word. We note what it costs, what it's genuinely good at, and — just as importantly — where it falls short, even when that's less exciting to write.

What we cover

  • Hands-on AI tool reviews, updated as products change
  • Side-by-side comparisons based on real tasks, not benchmarks alone
  • Practical productivity workflows you can set up in under an hour

How we make money

Primeloop is supported by display advertising and, occasionally, affiliate links clearly marked as such. Advertising never influences which tools we recommend or how we rate them — see our disclaimer for details.
